The Gamble

DIY Hanging

  • Bubbles, lifting seams, and misaligned repeats

  • Wall prep is usually skipped — and it shows
  • Ceilings and stairwells are genuinely difficult

  • Thin material that telegraphs every wall flaw

  • Meant to come down — not to last
How It Works

The Hillcrest wallpaper installation process

Wallpaper installation is all we do. Wall preparation and priming are included in every single installation, never sold as an extra.

01

Measure & Assess

We measure your walls, check their condition, and review your paper — its repeat, width, and how much the job needs — before anything is scheduled.

02

Prep & Prime

Included in every installation: old paper removed when selected, walls repaired, smoothed, and primed so your new paper bonds and lies flat.

03

Layout & Pattern Plan

We plan the first panel and every seam around the room — where the pattern starts, how it turns corners, and how it lands at doors and windows.

04

Hang & Smooth

Panels are cut, pasted, and hung by hand, then smoothed and rolled seam by seam for a flat, bubble-free finish.

05

Trim & Walkthrough

Edges are trimmed clean at ceilings, baseboards, and outlets, fixtures go back up, and we walk the room with you before we call it done.

Free in-home wallpaper installation consultation — we come to you
